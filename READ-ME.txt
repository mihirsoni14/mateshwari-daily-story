MATESHWARI JEWELLERS — STORY TEMPLATE SYSTEM
20 English templates | Instagram Stories | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16

OPEN THE SYSTEM
Open Mateshwari-Daily-Story-Editor.html in a current Chrome, Firefox, Edge,
or Safari browser. If it opens as code, use Open With > your browser.
The file contains the full editor, fonts and branding. It works offline.

FOUR CATEGORIES
DAILY RATES — 8 layouts
Traditional Royal; Modern Minimalist; Luxury Black & Gold; Elegant Ivory & Gold;
Heritage Indian; Contemporary Premium; Gold & Silver Split; Festive Jewellery.

DAILY MARKETING — 4 templates
Product Spotlight — add a photo, product name and description.
New Arrivals — introduce a collection with your photo and announcement.
Craftsmanship — highlight the detail and craft of your jewellery.
Visit the Showroom — add your address, contact and invitation.

FESTIVALS — 4 templates
Diwali Wishes; Dhanteras Wishes; Akshaya Tritiya; Navratri Wishes.
The English greetings and calls to action are editable. No festival date is

OFFERS — 4 templates
Featured Offer; Making Charges; Limited Period; Festive Offer.
Enter your approved benefit, eligible collection, expiry date and terms.
No discounts, selling prices or offer terms are invented or prefilled.

DAILY WORKFLOW
1. Choose a category and select a thumbnail card.
2. Select the Story date from the calendar.
3. Edit the fields shown for that template. Daily Rates shows the three prices;
   other categories show headlines, messages, calls to action, and offer fields.
4. For photo templates, upload a real JPG, PNG or WebP image, up to 20 MB.
5. Expand Saved business details, enter your contact, address and footer, then click
   Save business details once to reuse them whenever you reopen this browser.
6. Review the live preview, then click Download Story · PNG.
7. Upload the downloaded 1080 × 1920 image to Instagram or WhatsApp Stories.

PHOTO CONTROLS
Fit entire image keeps all of your photo visible. Fill the photo frame crops
the edges to fill the frame. The uploaded photo is shared by photo templates
while the editor stays open. Remove photo restores the placeholder.
Large photos are reduced to a maximum edge of 2048 pixels for efficient export.
Your image is processed in the browser; it is not uploaded to a service.

EDITING AND REUSE
The Story date and rate entries remain intact when switching categories.
Each marketing, festival and offer template keeps its own edited copy while
the editor is open. Your saved phone, showroom address and brand footer are shared
across templates. On returning to a template, your edits reappear. The last
selected template is remembered in browser storage where available.

SAVED BUSINESS DETAILS
Enter your phone/WhatsApp number, showroom address and preferred brand footer,
then click Save business details. These details remain available after closing
and reopening the editor in the same browser. They are stored locally on that
device and are not uploaded. Use Clear saved details to remove the saved profile.
The phone and footer appear wherever the layout allows; the address appears on
showroom templates.

Reopening the file starts a fresh Story session: date, prices, custom campaign
copy and photo are not restored. Saved business details remain available. This
avoids carrying stale rates or offers into a new Story while keeping your regular
showroom information ready.

Reset to placeholders in Daily Rates clears the date and all three rates.
Reset this template in other categories restores that template's default copy
and clears its offer fields, while keeping the shared date, photo and business
details. It does not clear edits to other templates.

PLACEHOLDERS AND DATES
Empty rates display ₹ XX,XXX. Gold is per 10 grams; silver is per kilogram.
Empty dates display [DD MMM YYYY]. Chosen dates use an English month abbreviation.
Empty product, contact and offer fields show labelled placeholders on the Story.
The offer expiry is a separate date picker from the Story date.
Long text wraps or reduces in size to stay within the designed text areas.

EXPORTS
PNG: 1080 × 1920, ready to post. The filename includes the template and date.
SVG: current template, editable text/decoration, embedded logo and uploaded photo.
SVG font support varies between applications. The HTML editor preserves its
embedded fonts when exporting PNG images.
